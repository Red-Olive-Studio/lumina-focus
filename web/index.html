      lumina

      A quiet light beside your work.

      Made for people who spend the whole day at a PC: developers, designers, writers, anyone deep in a screen.

      When you're in flow, the rest of the day disappears. The meeting slips by, the water glass stays full, and suddenly it's 7 pm with stiff shoulders. Lumina sits in the corner and keeps your day in view, so you can stay deep in the work without losing everything around it.

      • Now and next. Tasks, meals and your own calendar's meetings in one timeline, with a heads-up before each.
      • Focus that runs itself. Focus and breaks keep going all day, and History shows when you worked and when you rested.
      • Small nudges for your body. Water, posture, eyes, a walk. Nothing to tick off; they just pass by.
      • An evening that winds down. Warm light, a bedtime nudge, and a sleep timer for the late movie.

      Everything stays on this PC. No account, no tracking.
