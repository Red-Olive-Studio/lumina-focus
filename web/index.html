        İşinizin yanında sakin bir ışık.

        Your work pays well. Is your body getting paid back?

        You work on a screen, maybe earning thousands of dollars a month. Meanwhile the water glass stays full, your eyes haven't left the monitor since lunch, and it's 7 pm with stiff shoulders. Lumina keeps your health in the same view as your work: water, posture, eyes and a walk come by on their own, the screen turns warm in the evening, and bedtime gets a gentle nudge.

        One app for the whole workday.

        No juggling a focus timer, a to-do list, a calendar, a notes app and a night-light tool. It's all in one quiet timeline.

        • Your health, on time. Small nudges for water, posture, eyes, stretching and a walk. Nothing to tick off; they just pass by.
        • Now and next. Tasks, meals and your calendar's meetings in one timeline, with a heads-up before each and a Join button for the call.
        • Focus that runs itself. Focus and breaks keep going all day; History shows when you worked and when you rested.
        • Meeting notes. Take notes right on the meeting and find them again later.
        • An evening that winds down. Warm light, a bedtime nudge, and a sleep timer for the late movie.

        Everything stays on this device. No account, no tracking.
